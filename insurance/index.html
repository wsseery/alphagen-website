Business insurance

Business owner's policy (BOP)

General liability

Workers' compensation

Medical care and wage benefits for employees hurt on the job.

Commercial property

Landlord and small apartments

Restaurants

Professional liability (E&O)

Commercial umbrella

Extra liability limits above your underlying policies.

Builder's risk

Special events and entertainment

Liability for one-time events, festivals and productions.

Directors and officers (D&O)

Cars, vans and trucks your business owns, leases or uses.

Trucking and motor carriers

Liability, cargo and physical damage for trucks and trailers.

Inland marine and equipment

Surety bonds

Employment practices liability (EPLI)
