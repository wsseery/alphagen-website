Professional liability (E&O) insurance in Florida

Professional liability, also called errors and omissions (E&O), responds to claims that a mistake, oversight or failure in your professional services caused a client a financial loss. It fills a gap general liability does not reach.

AlphaGen Insurance Agency is an independent agency in Boca Raton, licensed in Florida, that compares several insurers for Florida businesses and households.

Who it's for

Consultants, accountants, designers, technology firms, real estate and insurance professionals, marketing agencies and other firms paid for advice or expertise.

Businesses whose client contracts require it.

What it can include

  • Defense costs — often the largest part of a professional claim; whether they reduce the limit depends on the form.
  • Settlements and judgments — for covered claims, up to the policy limit.
  • Prior acts — back to the retroactive date shown on the policy.
  • Extended reporting — an option to report claims after the policy ends, on claims-made forms.

Coverage depends on the policy. Nothing on this page describes what a specific policy will do: eligibility, underwriting and the terms, conditions and exclusions of the policy actually issued decide that.

What insurers ask for

To quote this line, an underwriter needs a picture of the risk. These are the items most insurers ask for. Not every insurer asks for every item, and some ask for more.

Business basics

  • Legal name, entity type and FEIN
  • Years in business and a description of operations
  • Locations and, where relevant, the class code that best describes the business — the class code sets which rating base and rate an underwriter uses

Exposure

  • Annual revenue and how it splits across the services you provide — the split shows how much of your work is in higher-risk services
  • Number of professionals and your largest clients

Line-specific

  • Your standard client contract, including any limitation-of-liability clause
  • Licenses and credentials
  • Your quality-control process
  • Prior professional liability coverage and its retroactive date

History

  • Past claims and any circumstances that could become a claim

Application forms: most insurers start from the industry-standard ACORD 825 Professional/Specialty Insurance Application, often with the ACORD 187 Professional Liability Supplement; many also use their own application or supplement.

Having your loss runs, inspections and current declarations ready usually speeds up a quote, because the underwriter can price from documents instead of waiting on follow-up questions.

Questions we hear

What does claims-made mean?

Most E&O policies respond to claims first made during the policy period, for work done after the retroactive date. Letting coverage lapse can leave earlier work uninsured.

Why does the retroactive date matter?

Work done before the retroactive date is not covered. When you change insurers, keep the original retro date if you can.

Is E&O the same as general liability?

No. General liability responds to bodily injury and property damage; E&O responds to financial loss caused by your professional services.

How much does professional liability insurance cost in Florida?

What drives the price is the business itself: what it does, its revenue or payroll, its location, its claims history, the limits and deductibles chosen, and the insurer's appetite for that class of business.
