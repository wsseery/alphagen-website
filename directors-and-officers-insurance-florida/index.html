Directors and officers (D&O) insurance in Florida

Directors and officers (D&O) insurance responds to claims that the people running an organization made wrongful management decisions. It protects the individuals, and on most forms the organization too.

AlphaGen Insurance Agency is an independent agency in Boca Raton, licensed in Florida, that compares several insurers for Florida businesses and households.

Who it's for

Private companies with a board or outside investors, nonprofits, and condominium and homeowners associations in Florida.

Founders raising capital, because investors often ask for it before joining a board.

What it can include

  • Side A — the directors and officers personally, when the organization cannot indemnify them.
  • Side B — reimbursement to the organization when it indemnifies them.
  • Side C — the organization itself, for certain claims, on many private-company forms.
  • Employment practices and fiduciary — sometimes packaged alongside D&O.

Coverage depends on the policy. Nothing on this page describes what a specific policy will do: eligibility, underwriting and the terms, conditions and exclusions of the policy actually issued decide that.

What insurers ask for

To quote this line, an underwriter needs a picture of the risk. These are the items most insurers ask for. Not every insurer asks for every item, and some ask for more.

Business basics

  • Legal name, entity type and FEIN
  • Years in business and a description of operations
  • Locations and, where relevant, the class code that best describes the business — the class code sets which rating base and rate an underwriter uses

Financials and ownership

  • Recent financial statements: assets, revenue, net income and debt — financial stress is one of the main triggers of claims against a board
  • Ownership, including any large shareholders, and the make-up of the board

Line-specific

  • Mergers, acquisitions, layoffs or financings in the past year or planned for the next
  • Changes in the chief executive or chief financial officer

History

  • Prior and pending litigation or investigations
  • Your current D&O insurer and its prior or pending litigation date

Application forms: most insurers start from the industry-standard ACORD 807 Directors and Officers Liability Section; most insurers also use their own application; many also use their own application or supplement.

Having your loss runs, inspections and current declarations ready usually speeds up a quote, because the underwriter can price from documents instead of waiting on follow-up questions.

Questions we hear

Does a small private company need D&O?

It depends on who could bring a claim: investors, lenders, competitors, regulators or employees. Many small companies buy it when they add outside investors or board members.

Is D&O claims-made?

Generally yes, so continuity and the retroactive or prior-acts date matter when you change insurers.

Does D&O cover bodily injury?

Generally no. Bodily injury and property damage are usually excluded and handled by general liability.

How much does D&O insurance cost in Florida?

What drives the price is the business itself: what it does, its revenue or payroll, its location, its claims history, the limits and deductibles chosen, and the insurer's appetite for that class of business.
