Special event and entertainment insurance in Florida

Special event insurance provides liability coverage for a single event or a series of events, such as a festival, concert, conference, wedding or production. Venues commonly require it, along with a certificate naming them.

AlphaGen Insurance Agency is an independent agency in Boca Raton, licensed in Florida, that compares several insurers for Florida businesses and households.

Who it's for

Event organizers, promoters, nonprofits, production companies and individuals holding an event at a venue that requires proof of insurance.

Vendors and exhibitors asked by an organizer to carry their own coverage.

What it can include

  • Event general liability — injuries and property damage to others during the event.
  • Liquor liability — when alcohol is served or sold, subject to the policy.
  • Additional insured status — for the venue, as many venues require.
  • Cancellation and equipment — available from some insurers as separate coverage.

Coverage depends on the policy. Nothing on this page describes what a specific policy will do: eligibility, underwriting and the terms, conditions and exclusions of the policy actually issued decide that.

What insurers ask for

To quote this line, an underwriter needs a picture of the risk. These are the items most insurers ask for. Not every insurer asks for every item, and some ask for more.

Event basics

  • Type of event, dates and number of days
  • Expected attendance per day, and whether it is indoors or outdoors
  • Your role: organizer, sponsor, vendor or host

Line-specific

  • Whether alcohol is served or sold, and by whom — alcohol is the single biggest change to an event's liability
  • Vendors and the certificates and hold-harmless agreements you collect from them
  • Rides, fireworks or other higher-risk activities
  • Security, crowd control and an emergency plan
  • The venue's insurance requirements

History

  • Prior years of the same event and any claims

Application forms: there is no common industry form for events; insurers use their own short applications.

Having your loss runs, inspections and current declarations ready usually speeds up a quote, because the underwriter can price from documents instead of waiting on follow-up questions.

Questions we hear

Does my venue's insurance cover my event?

Not usually for your own liability. Venues commonly require the organizer to carry its own policy and name the venue as an additional insured.

Is alcohol a problem?

It changes the questions, not always the answer. Insurers ask whether alcohol is served or sold and by whom.

How far ahead should I ask for a quote?

As early as you can, since some events need underwriter review.

How much does event insurance cost in Florida?

What drives the price is the business itself: what it does, its revenue or payroll, its location, its claims history, the limits and deductibles chosen, and the insurer's appetite for that class of business.
