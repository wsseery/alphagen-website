Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. This policy covers alphageninsurance.com and the quote and inquiry forms on it.

Who we are

AlphaGen Insurance Agency (“AlphaGen”, “we”, “us”) is a Florida-licensed independent insurance agency (2-20 General Lines, License G164863) located at 5550 Glades Road, Boca Raton, FL 33431. You can reach us at bill@alphageninsurance.com or (561) 220-0402.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, send an inquiry, or request one of our guides, you provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, the type of coverage you need, details about the property, business or vehicles to be insured, current coverage and claims history, and anything else you tell us in the notes. The forms on this site are our own: what you enter is sent to a Cloudflare Worker on our account, stored in our Cloudflare database and emailed to us. If we need documents from you — an inspection report, loss runs, a declarations page — we will ask for them by email or another secure channel rather than through the website.

Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived here. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers. Our hosting and network providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) also keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and performance. Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. When you submit a form we record the page you submitted from, the page that referred you, your browser type and the two-letter country your request came from — we do not store your IP address with your submission.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and this site is not directed to them.

How we use it

  • To respond to your quote request or inquiry and to send you the guide you asked for.
  • To obtain quotes and place coverage on your behalf with insurance carriers, managing general agents and wholesale brokers.
  • To service your policies once bound — renewals, changes, certificates and claims assistance.
  • To improve the website and measure whether our pages and forms work.
  • To meet our legal, licensing and record-keeping obligations as an insurance agency.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Insurance carriers and intermediaries — the companies we ask to quote or write your coverage. They receive only what is needed to underwrite and issue a policy, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • Service providers that run this site and our forms — Cloudflare (network, form processing, database, bot protection and email routing), Google (analytics and email) and GitHub (hosting). They process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own marketing.
  • When required by law — for example in response to a subpoena, a regulator’s request, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

How long we keep it

Quote requests and inquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and, if a policy is placed, for as long as Florida insurance record-keeping rules require. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings. You can ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.

Your choices

  • Analytics. You can block or clear cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Access, correction and deletion. Email bill@alphageninsurance.com and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it where we are not required to keep it.
  • Marketing email. If we send you anything beyond a reply to your request, every message includes an unsubscribe link.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel encrypted to us, and we limit access to your information to the people who need it to serve you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send Social Security numbers, full account numbers or other highly sensitive details through the website forms — if anything like that is needed, we will ask for it through a secure channel.

Links to other sites

Pages on this site may link to other websites, including our affiliate real estate site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date at the top.
