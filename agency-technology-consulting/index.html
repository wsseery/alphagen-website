For independent agencies

AI Agency Systems for Small Independent Agencies

We built an AI-assisted submission, carrier-tracking and comparative-quoting system to run our own Florida agency. Now we set up the same system for small independent P&C agencies, on your own appointments and in your own accounts.

What we run every day

Our carrier roster tracks 78 markets (insurers, MGAs, wholesalers and online raters) with the appetite, channel and appointment status of each one.

Every submission is logged by market, with a side-by-side quote comparison and a dashboard for status, win rate and days to quote. When a carrier appointment email arrives, an AI step reads it and updates the roster.

For quoting, one risk profile is shopped across every eligible market and channel we can reach, and every result is logged back to the tracker. A price-check mode does the same for a list of risks at once, using only markets that price online.

What your agency gets

  • A carrier and appetite roster loaded with your markets
  • A submissions log, quote comparison and dashboard
  • The AI carrier-onboarding step
  • A multi-market quoting workflow (optional)
  • Training and a written runbook
  • Monthly support (optional)

How it works

  1. Free workflow assessment. A 30-minute call on how you quote and track today, which markets you reach and where the time goes, followed by a one-page written recommendation.
  2. Setup. We build your tracker in your own Google account, load your appointed markets and quote-request templates, and train your team.
  3. Support, if you want it. New carriers added, appetite updates, fixes and a monthly working session.

What stays with you

  • Your data and accounts stay yours: the tracker, the AI account and every carrier login live in your agency’s accounts.
  • We never use our own appointments, agency codes or logins for your business.
  • Your licensed agents make every bind, signature and payment decision. The system drafts and logs; people decide.
  • The system uses AI (Claude), and your team reviews every output before it reaches a client or a carrier.
