About AlphaGen

Expertise in Complex Risks

AlphaGen Insurance Agency is a Florida-domiciled independent agency. We specialize in complex and large-scale risks, providing tailored coverage with deep Florida market expertise.

At AlphaGen Insurance Agency, we are committed to delivering customized insurance solutions that cater to the unique needs of our clients. Specializing in complex and larger risks, we work closely with businesses and property owners to protect their most valuable assets.

Whether you need coverage for commercial properties, multifamily developments, or high-value portfolios, AlphaGen provides expert guidance and tailored strategies to ensure comprehensive protection.

We also extend our services to homeowners and personal lines, offering competitive coverage for smaller risks such as residential properties. No matter the size or complexity of your insurance needs, we approach every policy with the same level of precision and expertise.

Headquartered in Boca Raton, AlphaGen draws on deep experience in the Florida insurance market — one of the most complex regulatory and catastrophe environments in the country. As a Florida-domiciled agency, we bring that expertise to commercial property, multifamily, workers’ compensation, and personal-lines placements wherever our clients operate.
